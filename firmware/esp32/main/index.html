Forward/backward polarity has been corrected for this chassis. Releasing a direction or losing commands stops the wheels.

Every servo starts at the nominal 1500 µs center and continuously receives PWM. Swing routines return all four joints to 1500 µs. Manual controls permit 500–2500 µs; approach unknown end stops slowly.
